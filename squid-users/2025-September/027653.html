On 2025-09-15 09:47, alex ' wrote:
> error: invalid conversion from 'const ASN1_STRING*' [-fpermissive]
> error: invalid conversion from 'const char*' to 'char*' [-fpermissive]

These errors are caused by an outdated OpenSSL declaration of 
ASN1_STRING_to_UTF8() and X509V3_EXT_conf_nid() functions. OpenSSL fixed 
those and many similar problems in 2016. Those fixes are available in 
OpenSSL v1.1.

Your options may include, without limitation:

* Upgrading to OpenSSL v1.1 or later
* Disabling the corresponding check using compiler flags

HTH,

Alex.
