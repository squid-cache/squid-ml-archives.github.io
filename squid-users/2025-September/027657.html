Dear Squid team,

According to the documentation at
https://www.squid-cache.org/Doc/config/dns_v4_first/
, the dns_v4_first configuration option is listed as being present in v5
and v6. However, in reality, this option was deprecated[1][2] and has had
no effect on Squid behavior since the v5 release.

Would it be possible to update the online documentation[3][4][5] to reflect
this?

[1] http://www.squid-cache.org/Versions/v5/RELEASENOTES.html#toc3.3
[2] https://www.squid-cache.org/Versions/v5/RELEASENOTES.html#toc2.2
[3] https://www.squid-cache.org/Versions/v5/cfgman/dns_v4_first.html
[4] https://www.squid-cache.org/Versions/v6/cfgman/dns_v4_first.html
[5] https://www.squid-cache.org/Doc/config/dns_v4_first/

Thank you for your help.
