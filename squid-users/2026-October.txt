Not afaik in Squid. This may be of interest though: OpenBSD dropped
"emSign ECC Root CA - C3" (the first line of the b64-encoded cert
is "MIICKzCCAbGgAwIBAgIKe3G2gla4EnycqDAKBggqhkjOPQQDAzBaMQswCQYDVQQG")
and replaced it with a modified version of the not-distrusted
intermediates. As a result, certs signed with the various eMudhra
and InCommon ECC intermediates are still verifiable.

>From new OpenBSD cert.pem, those intermediates are:


### eMudhra intermediates re-issued as local anchors (emSign ECC Root CA - G3 removed)
###
### These were added as elided intermediates in response to the removal
### of the eMudhra G3 root which had 8 compromised intermediates
### with trivial keys, (such as '4') as of 2026-10-20. These
### are intermediates promoted to roots by making their issuer
### be themselves, (the same way netscape does this). the signatures
### will not match but are not checked, so this is fine.
### These roots should be removed in a future release, likely
### within a year. - beck@
